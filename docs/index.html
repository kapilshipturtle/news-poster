Privacy policy: news-poster

Last updated: October 21, 2026

news-poster is an internal tool that publishes news posts to one Facebook Page managed by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the Page's own administrators.

What data the app uses

The app uses a Page access token, granted by the Page administrator, only to publish posts to that Page and to read back its own posts. It does not request, collect, store or share personal data from Facebook users, visitors, commenters or followers.

What data the app stores

Only a log of its own published posts (time, headline, post ID and the public news sources used). No personal data about any person is stored.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no personal data about users, there is nothing to delete. To ask a question or request deletion of anything you believe relates to you, open an issue at github.com/kapilshipturtle/news-poster/issues and it will be handled within 30 days.
